A little guide to borrowing, reading and caring for our books.

Ulooma is a small library built around a simple idea: making room for more reading in our everyday lives.

We want to build a quiet, intentional culture of reading around us. Something simple, warm and consistent.

The structure is here to keep things running smoothly, but the heart of it is care: for the books, for the people who read them, and for the little community we're building together.

If you'd like to be part of this little library, here's how it works.

membership

Ulooma is a small, members-only library, currently serving readers in Lagos.

Membership slots open once every quarter so we can keep the library small and manageable.

Once you register your interest, we'll review your application and send you an invitation within two weeks. Invitations are sent to accepted applicants on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to available membership slots.

We'll also announce new membership openings through our Substack and Instagram.

Membership comes with a yearly commitment fee of ₦10,000, which goes towards keeping the library sustainable, organised and running.

Once your membership is confirmed and payment is received, we'll arrange for delivery of your library card. Yes, it's a physical card.

Registering your interest doesn't automatically guarantee membership. If you're invited to join, you'll need to pay the commitment fee within the stated period to secure your spot.

If you'd like to join us, register to become a member

Membership renews yearly. If your membership expires, borrowing privileges will pause until you renew.

browsing and borrowing

Our library catalogue and borrowing system are hosted on The Northstar Library.

Once your membership is confirmed, you'll receive access to browse the collection, check book availability and submit borrowing requests.

Here's how borrowing works:

  • You can borrow up to three books at a time.
  • You can keep up to five books on hold for when they become available.
  • If a book is already on loan, join its hold queue. You'll receive a notification when it's your turn.
  • When requesting an available book, select your preferred pickup date and expected return date through the platform.
  • Once your request is approved, we'll share the collection details.

Please borrow books you genuinely intend to read. No collecting books just to admire them on your shelf. 😭

pickup and collection

Books can be collected from our designated pickup location in Yaba, Lagos.

You have three options for getting your books:

  • Self-pickup: Come collect your books yourself.
  • Your own dispatch: Arrange for a rider to collect your books on your behalf.
  • Library-arranged dispatch: Let us arrange a rider for you. We'll communicate the delivery cost and arrangements beforehand. Delivery cost is on you.

Whichever option you choose, please communicate your plans ahead of time so we can coordinate collection without any confusion.

Our pickup address is shared privately with confirmed members.

If you're outside Lagos, forgive us until we figure out how to make this work. 🫶🏾

reading and returns

We ask that you return borrowed books within 2–4 weeks, ideally by the return date you selected when making your request.

This gives you enough time to enjoy your books while allowing someone else to read them too.

If you need a little more time, please reach out before your return date. We'll do our best to accommodate you, depending on whether someone else is waiting for the book.

We'll also send a gentle reminder when your return date is approaching.

When returning books, please coordinate with us so we can arrange its collection or drop-off.

taking care of the books

Our books are shared, which means every reader has a little responsibility towards the next.

Please return books in the same condition in which you received them.

A few things we'd like you to avoid:

  • Writing, underlining or highlighting pages.
  • Using pens, markers or sticky tabs.
  • Eating or drinking around open books in ways that could cause stains or damage.
  • Folding pages or damaging book covers.
  • Making any alterations that affect the book's condition.

We want each book to pass through different hands and still feel lovely to read.

If a book is accidentally damaged, please let us know. If a book is lost or damaged beyond repair, you'll be responsible for replacing it or covering the replacement cost.

We work with shared responsibility. 🧡

sharing the library

Ulooma is a members-only space, and membership is personal.

Books borrowed from the library should remain with the member who checked them out. Please don't lend, transfer or pass them along to someone else outside the library.

If you know someone who would enjoy being part of Ulooma, we're happy to take referrals. Just send them our way!

staying in touch

Communication makes everything easier.

Please keep us informed about:

  • Your preferred pickup and return arrangements.
  • Any changes to your collection plans.
  • Delays or requests for an extension.
  • Lost, damaged or misplaced books.
  • Any other issues that come up along the way.

You can always reach out to us. We'd rather know what's happening than have you worry about figuring things out on your own.

A little reminder before you go

This library works because we all play a part in keeping it going.

Read the books. Return them with care. Make room for someone else to enjoy them.

And most importantly, have a lovely time reading.

We're happy you're here.

With love,
Lulu & Ore
