This is a small, members-only library, currently serving only in Lagos.

Membership slots open once per quarter so we can keep the library small and manageable. Once you register your interest, we'll review your application and send you an invitation within 2 weeks. We'll also communicate when new slots open through our substack and instagram.

Ụlọọma's Library partners with The Northstar Library. This means your membership gives you access to books from both libraries, with over 1,200 titles to explore across our collections.

Please note that registering your interest does not guarantee membership.

If you're invited to join, you'll receive a library card after paying a yearly commitment fee of ₦10,000. This fee goes towards keeping the library sustainable, structured, and taken seriously.

Once you're in, you can borrow a maximum of three(3) books at a time. Read it, bring it back, then take another. There's no pressure, but we ask that you return the books within 2-4 weeks so books can keep moving around.

At the heart of it, we just want more books to be read, loved, and shared. We hope you'll find something special in this little community we're building.

If you have any questions or need to reach us, email library@ulooma.co.
